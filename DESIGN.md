# Design Brief — Manya Destination | Premium Himalayan Travel

## Differentiation
Premium, refined Himalayan adventure platform inspired by Apple.com minimalism — generous whitespace, elegant Cormorant Garamond display typography, warm earthy palette. Cream navbar (#F5ECD7) with Mahogany accents. Authoritative, soulful, expert-driven for luxury travelers.

## Color Palette (CSS Variables)
| Use | Variable | Hex Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Brand | --color-black-bean | #3C1414 | Navigation, primary CTAs, headings |
| Secondary Accent | --color-mahogany | #C04000 | Hover states, secondary highlights |
| Alert/Accent | --color-cadmium-orange | #ED872D | Price tags, alerts, featured badges |
| Premium Badge | --color-gold | #E6BE8A | Dividers, premium badges, icons |
| Navbar Background | --color-cream | #F5ECD7 | Header background, light surfaces |
| Primary Background | --bg-primary | #FAFAF7 | Main page background |
| Secondary Background | --bg-secondary | #F5F0E8 | Card backgrounds |
| Text Primary | --text-primary | #1A1A1A | Body copy |

## Typography
| Role | Font | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Display/Hero | Cormorant Garamond | H1-H2, trek names, editorial headlines |
| Body/UI | Inter | Body copy, buttons, nav, forms |
| Data | JetBrains Mono | Altitude, pricing, duration stats |

## Layout & Zones
| Zone | Background | Font | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navbar | Cream #F5ECD7 | Inter | Mahogany text, white CTA buttons |
| Hero | Primary off-white | Cormorant Garamond | Centered, italic, generous whitespace |
| Featured Adventures | Primary | Cormorant/Inter | Horizontal or grid, price badges visible |
| Trek/Yatra Cards | Secondary | Inter | Orange decorative accents, minimal styling |
| Footer | Tertiary | Inter | 4 columns, expanded details, trust marks |

## Elevation & Depth
Cards: 0 2px 8px rgba(60,20,20,0.06); Hover: 0 8px 24px rgba(60,20,20,0.10); Modals: 0 20px 60px rgba(60,20,20,0.15)

## Component Patterns
Primary CTA: Black Bean bg, white text; Secondary CTA: white bg, Black Bean text; Price Badge: Cadmium Orange, round corners; Cards: warm off-white, Gold top divider

## Motion
Transitions: 300ms ease-out; Scroll: smooth fade-in; Hover: shadow increase, 5% darken

## Structural Constraints
Max-width: 1400px; Mobile-first single column; Generous padding (1.5rem mobile, 4rem desktop); Minimal borders; 4px max border-radius

## Signature Details
Cormorant Garamond italic in hero; Cream navbar with Mahogany text; Gold accent lines above section headers; Warm tones throughout; Generous whitespace between sections
